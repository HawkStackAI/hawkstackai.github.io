Mahjong Match

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

The short version: Mahjong Match collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your games, progress and settings stay on your device.

Who we are

Mahjong Match is published by the developer named on its App Store listing ("we"). This policy explains what the Mahjong Match app for iPhone and iPad does and does not do with information.

Information we collect

None. We do not operate any server that the app talks to. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not collect device identifiers or usage analytics.

Information stored on your device

To work as a game, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • your in-progress and completed games, including moves and elapsed time;
  • your statistics, such as games played and won, win rate by grade, best times and daily streaks;
  • your settings, such as tile design, sounds and appearance;
  • whether Mahjong Match Pro has been unlocked.

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it. If you use iCloud device backups, Apple may include this data in your backup under Apple's own terms.

Purchases

Mahjong Match Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple provides the app with a receipt so it can unlock Pro features and restore your purchase on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Mahjong Match is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party software development kits for advertising, analytics or tracking. The only third party involved in your use of the app is Apple, as the operator of the App Store and in-app purchases.

Your choices

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You can remove all app data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting information, we will update this policy before that version ships and change the effective date above.
