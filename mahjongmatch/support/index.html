I bought Mahjong Match Pro, but it looks locked

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, and restoring brings Pro back on any device signed in to the same Apple ID, at no extra charge. Restore Purchases is also on the purchase screen itself.

What does Pro include?

One one-time purchase unlocks 12 more board layouts (24 in total), 4 more tile designs (Jade, Porcelain, Sandalwood and Night), the full daily archive, and full statistics. It is not a subscription and it never renews. The daily board, 12 layouts, and unlimited hints and shuffles are free for everyone, forever, and there are no ads in the app for anyone.

How do hints and shuffles work?

They are free and unlimited for everyone. A hint highlights a playable pair. A shuffle re-deals the tiles you have left into an arrangement that can still be finished. Help is never the product.

What happens when no moves are left?

The app tells you plainly, and offers a free shuffle or undo. Every board is generated from a proven solution and checked before you see it, so there is always a way through.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refund requests go to Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Our Privacy Policy has the details.
