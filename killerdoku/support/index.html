I bought KillerDoku Pro, but it looks locked

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, and restoring brings Pro back on any device signed in to the same Apple ID, at no extra charge. Restore Purchases is also on the purchase screen itself.

What does Pro include?

One one-time purchase unlocks the full library of 1,000+ puzzles, the cage combination helper, unlimited hints, and full statistics. It is not a subscription and it never renews. The Daily Puzzle and the starter library are free for everyone, forever, and there are no ads in the app for anyone.

How do hints work?

Every puzzle includes 3 teaching hints for free. A hint names the technique and shows it on your board, so you learn as you solve. Pro makes hints unlimited.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your daily streak is protected by streak freezes for the days life gets in the way. Your progress, statistics and settings are stored on your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refund requests go to Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Our Privacy Policy has the details.
