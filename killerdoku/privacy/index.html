KillerDoku

Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

The short version: KillerDoku collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your puzzles, progress and settings stay on your device.

Who we are

KillerDoku is published by the developer named on its App Store listing ("we"). This policy explains what the KillerDoku app for iPhone and iPad does and does not do with information.

Information we collect

None. We do not operate any server that the app talks to. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not collect device identifiers or usage analytics.

Information stored on your device

To work as a puzzle game, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • your progress in each puzzle, including entries, notes, elapsed time, hints used and mistakes;
  • your statistics, such as puzzles solved, best times and daily streaks;
  • your settings, such as play style, timer visibility, haptics and reminder preferences;
  • whether KillerDoku Pro has been unlocked, and the app version in which we last asked you to rate the app.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it (see Sharing below). Deleting the app deletes it. If you use iCloud device backups, Apple may include this data in your backup under Apple's own terms.

Purchases

KillerDoku Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple provides the app with a receipt so it can unlock Pro features and restore your purchase on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the Daily Puzzle reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification service of ours is involved, and nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

When you tap Share after a solve, the app creates an image and a line of text describing your result (level, time, hints used and, for the Daily Puzzle, your streak) and hands them to the iOS share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app you share to. The card contains no personal information.

Rating prompt

The app may occasionally ask you to rate it using Apple's standard rating prompt. Apple decides whether and when the prompt appears, and any rating or review you leave is submitted to Apple, not to us.

Children

KillerDoku is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party software development kits for advertising, analytics or tracking. The only third party involved in your use of the app is Apple, as the operator of the App Store, in-app purchases and the rating prompt.

Your choices

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You can remove all app data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting information, we will update this policy before that version ships and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@killerdoku.app.
